# AI LOG - Sprint 1

## AI Tool Used

ChatGPT

## Date

September 22, 2026

## Purpose

Help me understand and complete the different sections of the Sprint 1 Project Management document, including the Sprint Goal, Sprint Backlog, Priorities, Effort Estimation, Team Capacity, and Risks. ChatGPT was also used to improve the wording and organization of my contributions while keeping the information based on my team's actual work.

## Prompts Used

1. **"I need to do the Sprint planning and these are the different sections, can you give me a description of what i have to write?"**

   * Used ChatGPT to understand what information should be included in each Sprint Planning section.

2. **"These are the issues we have or the user stories: [US-01 to US-15]"**

   * Used ChatGPT to understand the difference between the overall Product Backlog and the Sprint 1 Backlog and determine which user stories should be included in Sprint 1.

3. **"Wait so they just worked on the first two, so i just leave the first two?"**

   * Confirmed that only US-01 User Registration and US-02 User Login should be included in the Sprint 1 Backlog because these were the user stories selected for the sprint.

4. **"### Sprint Goal ... Is this good for the goal?"**

   * Used ChatGPT to review and improve the wording of the Sprint Goal describing the implementation of the web application's registration and login functionality.

5. **"Ok now for effort estimation what exactly should i put on it"**

   * Used ChatGPT to understand how effort estimation works and how to represent the effort required for US-01 and US-02.

6. **"### Team Capacity [draft]"**

   * Used ChatGPT to improve the description of the team's capacity and explain how the six team members were divided into pairs for front-end, back-end, and documentation tasks.

7. **"### Risks - Not finishing on time for submission - Back-end and front-end not merging correctly"**

   * Used ChatGPT to identify mitigation strategies for the risks related to completing the sprint on time and integrating the front-end and back-end.

8. **"### Effort Estimation [draft]"**

   * Used ChatGPT to correct and clarify the effort estimation scale from 1 to 5 and explain why User Registration and User Login were both assigned an effort of 3.

9. **"Can I put something like, although both tasks, user registration and login, need back end work..."**

   * Used ChatGPT to help formulate an explanation justifying the estimated effort for the two user stories.

## AI Contribution

ChatGPT was used as a support tool to clarify Scrum and Sprint Planning concepts, organize the information, improve grammar and wording, and provide suggestions for presenting the team's work. The final decisions regarding the Sprint 1 tasks, priorities, workload distribution, effort estimates, and risks were based on the team's actual work and discussions.
